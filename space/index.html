The cairn

A cairn is a pile of stones that travelers add to as they pass, so that the next person can see the way has been walked before. The one at the center of this cluster has a small light on it, which is kept burning through the night.

Whoever passes can leave a constellation: a figure of three to seven systems, linked with lines and given a name. Leaving one adds your address to the allowlist for the mint.
