2023 · 77 pieces

curtains

‘Curtains’ is a collection of 77 hand-made pieces, bound together by their coherent aesthetics and consistent compositions. Their style may be evocative of generative art collections, where a number of parameters are mixed and matched to generate different outputs, and that is on purpose — ‘Curtains’ was made with gen art as its main inspiration, as a form to consolidate the familiar wave patterns, layered compositions, and color palettes present throughout my work.

The collection is composed by seven different palettes (Night, Haze, Garden, Classic, Volcano, Mono, and Neue), three different orientations (Vertical, Horizontal, and Diagonal), and three coloring techniques (Shaded, Flat, and Gradients), mixed and matched to create the many outputs seen below. The variation is enough to make the collection interesting to delve into, without becoming repetitive.
